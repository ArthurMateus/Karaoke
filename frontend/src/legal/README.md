# Footer pages shown in the app

Copies of the legal drafts in `docs/legal/drafts/` (written by the legal reviewer agent), shown at
`/terms`, `/privacy` and `/credits`. Changed for the app: `credits.md` links the full licence texts in
`frontend/public/licenses/` and drops the developer notes at its end. When a draft in `docs/legal/drafts/`
changes, copy it here again. They stay marked as drafts until a lawyer has reviewed them.
