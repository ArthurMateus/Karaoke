# Privacy Policy

> **Beta version, a draft for a lawyer to review.** This policy describes the private Encore beta. The Portuguese version is the main one.

Privacy Policy for the beta, version 1, 20 October 2026.

## Who is responsible

{{operator}}, an individual in {{city}}, Brazil, is the controller of your personal data under the LGPD (Lei 13.709/2018). Contact for privacy requests: {{contact}}. As a small processing agent (ANPD Resolution 2/2022) we haven't named a data protection officer (encarregado); write to the contact above instead.

## The beta

- Encore is in a **private, invite-only beta**: only invited emails can create an account, and guests join only through a friend's room.
- It's for people aged **18 or older**.
- The beta ends on {{beta_end}}. When it ends, **we delete all beta data** (accounts, songs, recordings, scores) within 30 days, and we tell you by email first.
- During the beta only the operator has access to the server and the database.

## What we collect and why

| What | Why | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| **Email address** (accounts) | To send your sign-in link and identify your account | Performing our contract with you |
| **Display name** (accounts and guests) | Shown to people in your rooms, in results, and on the song-of-the-day leaderboard | Contract |
| **Your songs**: the audio you upload, the lyrics you paste or pick, the chart | To convert them and play them for you and the rooms you host | Contract |
| **Recordings of your singing** (rooms with group replays, solo, song of the day) | To make replays, and to check top-10 song-of-the-day scores by listening to the recording again | Contract and legitimate interest (fair leaderboards) |
| **Live voice** (Spotlight) | Streamed live to the stage screen and listeners in the room; not recorded | Contract |
| **Scores, votes, reactions, personal bests, plan and codes** | To run the game and your plan | Contract |
| **Acceptance of the beta terms** (version and time) | To show you agreed to the terms | Contract |
| **Sign-in session** (a cookie) and **technical logs** (IP address, time, page requested) | To keep you signed in and protect the service | Contract and legitimate interest |

We don't use your voice to identify you, we don't sell your data, and we don't show ads.

## Who can see what

- **People in a room:** your name, ready state, scores, reactions, and, in rooms with group replays, the replay that mixes everyone's voices. In a room you host, the people in it can hear your uploaded songs.
- **Anyone with the link:** names and scores on the song-of-the-day leaderboard (including yesterday's winner).
- **Only you:** your email, your song library, and your plan details (grants, codes, Night Passes waiting). When you host a room, the people in it see whether it has premium features and, during a Night Pass, the time it ends.
- **The operator**, as the person who runs the server.
- **Our service providers**, only to run the app: hosting and file storage: {{hosting}}; sign-in email delivery: {{email_provider}}. When a provider is outside Brazil, we use the safeguards the ANPD requires for international transfers (standard contractual clauses, Resolution 19/2024).
- **LRCLIB** (lrclib.net): when you search for lyrics, our server sends the song title, artist and length. No personal data is sent, and your device doesn't contact LRCLIB.
- Authorities, when the law requires it.

## How long we keep it

- Recordings and replays: **30 days** after the song, then deleted. The score stays.
- Guest records (name, scores, recordings): **30 days** after they were created.
- Sign-in links: 15 minutes; sessions: 30 days. Expired links and sessions are deleted at the weekly clean-up.
- Server logs with IP addresses: **14 days**, then deleted.
- Uploaded songs and your account: until you delete them, ask us to, or the beta ends.
- **Everything from the beta**: deleted within 30 days after the beta ends.

## Cookies and storage on your device

We use **one cookie**, the sign-in session. It is needed for the app to work, so we don't ask for consent. The app also saves a few settings in your browser (language, your microphone delay, and the "listen from afar" and stage voice switches) so you don't have to set them again. We use no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. Fonts are served from our own server.

## Your rights

You can ask us to confirm whether we process your data, see it, correct it, get a copy, delete it, learn who we share it with, and withdraw consent where we rely on it (LGPD art. 18). You can delete your songs on the Songs page. To delete your account and all its data, or for anything else, write to {{contact}} from your account's email (guests: tell us your name and the room). **We delete your account and data within 15 days** and confirm by email. You can also complain to the ANPD (gov.br/anpd).

## Children

The beta is for adults only (18 or older). If you think a child has used it, write to {{contact}} and we'll delete the data.

## Security

Sign-in uses one-time links (no passwords), sessions are stored as hashes, and connections use HTTPS. If a security incident could harm you, we'll tell you and the ANPD.

## Changes

We'll update the version and date above and tell you by email about important changes.
