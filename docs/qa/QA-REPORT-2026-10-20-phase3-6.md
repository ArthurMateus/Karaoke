## Not tested, and why

- Reduced-motion rendering (the browser pane can't emulate `prefers-reduced-motion`; I checked the code instead).
- The clipboard "Copied" state: the pane denies clipboard write permission (`NotAllowedError`), so the app correctly falls back to selecting the code.
- "Listen from afar" audio through LiveKit, real phones, a real TV and real microphones (robot only).
- Yesterday's daily winner card (no data). "Find lyrics online" (it calls an outside lyrics service; I skipped it).
- Phases 1-2 flows and error flows (wrong code, full room, kicked, closed room, offline banner): out of scope for this run.
- Stage screenshots are low resolution because the pane scales a 1920x1080 view down. I measured text sizes and positions in the DOM instead.
